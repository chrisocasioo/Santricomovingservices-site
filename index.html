Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book?

For moves, we recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead, especially for weekends and the busy summer season. Junk removal and furniture delivery can often be scheduled within a few days. Reach out anytime and we'll do our best to fit you in.

How is pricing determined?

Moves are priced by size, distance, and crew needed. Junk removal is based on how much we haul away. Delivery and assembly depend on the number and type of items. You always get a clear, upfront quote before we start.

What kinds of junk do you take?

Most household and office items: furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, boxes, and general clutter. Tell us what you have when requesting a quote and we'll confirm.

Can you pick up furniture I bought from a store or online?

Yes. We can pick up from stores, warehouses, or marketplace sellers, deliver it to your home, carry it to the room you want, and assemble it on the spot.

Do you assemble furniture I already have delivered?

Absolutely. If your furniture has already arrived in boxes, we can come out just to assemble it and haul away the packaging.

Do you provide packing materials for moves?

Yes. We can supply boxes, tape, and wrapping, or fully pack your home for you. Furniture blankets and floor protection are always included.
