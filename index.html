Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should I book my move?

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead, especially for weekends and the busy summer season. That said, reach out anytime — we'll do our best to accommodate last-minute moves.

How is the cost of my move determined?

Pricing depends on the size of your move, distance, crew size, and any extra services such as packing or specialty items. We provide a clear, upfront quote before moving day.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes. We can supply boxes, tape, wrapping, and padding, or fully pack your home for you. Furniture blankets and floor protection are always included.

Are my belongings protected during the move?

Absolutely. We are fully insured, and our crew uses professional wrapping and securing techniques to keep your belongings safe in transit.

Do you move specialty items like pianos or antiques?

Yes — just let us know when requesting your quote so we can bring the right equipment and plan accordingly.
